# Project Brief

## 1. Background
Since I started taking photos, I have primarily stored them on my personal computer and other storage devices. However, I have always been concerned about losing these memories due to hard drive failures, device loss, or accidentally disposing of a storage device.

To reduce this risk, I began uploading my photos to social media as a way to preserve memories and share them with others. I also enjoyed the experience of documenting my everyday life and connecting with others through photography.

However, while social media platforms are useful for documenting and sharing everyday moments, they are not designed to preserve and manage the detailed information associated with each photo.

This led me to the idea of creating a service for people who enjoy taking and viewing photos—a service that not only allows users to preserve their memories, but also securely backs up their photos to the cloud along with detailed information such as the date and location of capture, camera, lens, and shooting settings.

## 2. Objective
The primary objective of this project is to provide a reliable and user-friendly service for preserving and managing personal photographs in the cloud.

The service aims to:
・Safely preserve photos by automatically backing them up to the cloud.
・Preserve detailed photo information, including metadata such as capture date, location, camera, lens, and shooting settings.
・Help users organize and revisit their memories by allowing them to explore their photos together with the context and information associated with them.
・Provide a personal archive for photography enthusiasts that goes beyond simple photo storage and sharing.

## 3. Scope

### In Scope
- User registration and authentication
- Photo upload and cloud backup
- Photo metadata extraction and storage
- Photo browsing and viewing
- Photo search and filtering
- Photo organization and categorization
- Selective photo sharing with trusted people
- Viewing shared photos and their associated information
- Access control for shared content

### Out of Scope
- Photo printing
- Photo editing
- Likes, comments, and follower systems
- Third-party cloud storage integration

## 4. Risks
- Cloud storage costs may increase as the number of uploaded photos grows.
- Large photo uploads may require significant storage and network bandwidth.
- Photo metadata may be missing or inconsistent depending on the camera or image format.
- Data loss or corruption could occur during photo upload or migration.
- Unauthorized access to private photos could pose a security risk.
